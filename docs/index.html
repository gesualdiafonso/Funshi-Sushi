    How ordering works

    No app and no account. Your order goes straight to us on WhatsApp.

    1. Choose your dishes

      Browse the menu and add what you'd like. Your selection stays saved on this device.

    2. Send it on WhatsApp

      Add your name and choose delivery or pickup. We write the order message for you; you press send.

    3. We confirm and prepare

      We reply on WhatsApp to confirm the total and timing, then start preparing your order.
