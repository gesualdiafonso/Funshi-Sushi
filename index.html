How we work

Four ideas behind every order.

  • The fish leads

    The menu follows what is good and in season, rather than the other way around.

  • Rice is half the piece

    Seasoned, shaped by hand and served at the right temperature, because it carries the flavour as much as the fish does.

  • A short menu, done well

    Fewer dishes, each one made the same careful way every time.

  • Ordering without friction

    Clear prices, a few taps, and a real person at the restaurant confirming your order.
